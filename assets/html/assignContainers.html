Assign Containers

Once you're on-site (or LS-CAT staff are loading on your behalf), containers need to be assigned to positions in the beamline's sample-handling robot before data collection can start.

Before you start

You'll need a shipment created and samples registered first — see Create a Shipment and Registering Samples.

  1. Go to your current visit and click Assign Containers in the top left.
  2. ISPyB/Synchweb shows the beamline's sample changer alongside your available dewars and containers. On 21-ID-D and 21-ID-F this is the UniPuck sample changer and 21-ID-G is being upgraded to a UniPuck sample changer.
  3. Drag each container onto its intended position on the sample changer diagram.
  4. Confirm the placement when prompted (click Ok).
  5. In MXCuBE3, click Refresh to pull your container assignments in from ISPyB/Synchweb.

Remote sessions

For remote beamtime, your Local Contact typically handles the physical loading and container assignment for you — these steps are mainly useful for double-checking assignments or for on-site, self-loading visits.
