Getting Beamtime At LS-CAT

  1. Please read our Terms of Service. By using LS-CAT's facilities, you are agreeing to them.

  2. Please send a request to beamtime@ls-cat.org with "BEAMTIME REQUEST" in the subject line, and we will help you find the best available timeslot for your needs.

  3. Free Access for General Users: Please submit a General User Proposal with a Beam Time Request for LS-CAT Beamline 21-ID-D, 21-ID-F, or 21-ID-G as your first choice.

Safety

  • Please read LS-CAT's official safety plan.

  • LS-CAT and the APS rely on Integrated Safety Management (learn more here).

  • On-site users: Experiment Safety Assessment Forms are due
    7 days BEFORE you arrive on-site.

Remote Interface (December 2025)

  • LS-CAT is migrating from lsnode.ls-cat.org to MXCuBE3 remote data collection software. This upgrade will support use of the MD3 diffractometer and UniPuck sample changer, allowing for both sample rastering and fully-automated data collection.
  • MXCuBE3 will be first deployed on ID-D during 2026-1, and then ID-F and ID-G will be upgraded with MD3-UP diffractometers, UniPuck sample changers, and MXCuBE3 controls.
