Data Retrieval

LS-CAT retains our data on-site for 30 days following the end of the experimental data collection date and uses Globus to manage access to data. Our Globus GCSv5 collection can be found by logging into Globus using your Argonne National Laboratory identity and clicking the following link:

  • https://app.globus.org/file-manager/collections/c76575d2-8574-450a-bd40-3030453bd852

Please note the following:

  1. The globus.org UUID for our Globus GCSv5 endpoint ("LS-CAT Data") is:
    c76575d2-8574-450a-bd40-3030453bd852

  2. You must select Argonne National Laboratory as your identity provider to access your data in Globus. Your ANL identity must be linked to your Globus account. Please note that this is the also same login mechanism and identity you used to log into LS-CAT's mxcube3-based remote interface.

Troubleshooting:

If you are unable to log into LS-CAT's mxcube3-based remote interface or download your data via Globus, please send an email to administrator@ls-cat.org with the following information:

  1. The ESAF #

  2. Your APS Badge #
