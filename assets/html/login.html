Please access the remote data collection interface via CILogon by clicking the redirect button below:

Please note the following:

  1. To download your data, please log into globus.org and access the Globus GCSv5 collection named "LS-CAT Data".

  2. In CILogon, you must select ORCiD as your identity provider to access the remote interface, and your email address must be verified.

  3. Your CILogon account must use the same institutional email address you provided for your Experimental Safety Assessment Form (ESAF).

  4. In ORCiD, the visibility of your email address must be set to "Everyone" or "Trusted", and CILogon must be enabled under "Trusted Parties".

  5. Most Mondays LS-CAT remote services are unavailable for a few hours to perform routine maintenance.

Troubleshooting:

If you are unable to log into the remote interface or download your data via Globus, please send an email to administrator@ls-cat.org with the following information:

  1. The ESAF #

  2. Your APS Badge #

  3. Your ORCiD ID #
