Mobile Access

SynchWeb's interface adapts to smaller screens, so you can check on a remote session from a phone or tablet without needing the full desktop layout.

What you can do

Register shipments and samples, and follow data collections in real time, from a phone or tablet browser.

What changes

Menus reorganize for the smaller screen, but nearly all of SynchWeb's functionality remains reachable on mobile.

Good for

Keeping an eye on an overnight or unattended session, or checking processing results while away from the beamline workstation — not a substitute for the full desktop view when you're actively setting up a collection.
