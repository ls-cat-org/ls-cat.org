Pins, Caps, and Vials for LS-CAT

Pins

LS-CAT supports 18mm pins for both robotic and manual mounting. This results in a distance of 22mm from the bottom of the base to the sample.

Critical: If pins are too long or short, your sample cannot be positioned in the cryostream or the X-ray beam.

Magnetism & Maintenance

We rely on magnetic properties for mounting. Caps with weak or reversed magnetism will cause samples to fall during transfer.

Pro Tip: Always remove rusty or damaged caps from service immediately to prevent robot failures.
