Registering a Protein

Proteins have to be registered and approved before you can use them when registering samples or building a shipment.

Registration

Register your protein through LS-CAT's protein/proposal registration process. Once approved, it becomes available in ISPyB/Synchweb for shipping and data collection.

Finding it in ISPyB/Synchweb

Select Proteins from the proposal dropdown menu to see your registered proteins. Approved proteins show a green label; use the filter box to search by name.

Names must match exactly

Protein names in your registration and in ISPyB/Synchweb have to match. If they don't, your samples won't be available when you go to build a new container.

Add a sequence or a PDB file

Supplying extra information about your protein unlocks automated analysis after data collection:

  • Sequence — enables automated molecular-replacement (MrBUMP) and lets a predicted structure be generated (e.g. via AlphaFold, where enabled) ahead of your visit.
  • PDB file — enables automated refinement (DIMPLE) once data has been collected.

Cloning a protein

If you're working with a variant of a protein you've already registered — a selenomethionine derivative or a point mutant, for example — select the approved protein, click Clone Protein, edit the details, and click Add Protein to save the new entry.
