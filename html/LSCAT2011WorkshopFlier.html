2011 LS-CAT Symposium/Workshop

March 22-23, 2011

Advanced Photon Source, LS-CAT Sector 21

This year our symposium will focus on the research programs of various LS-CAT member institutions, new advancements at the LS-CAT beamlines, and the coming BioNanoprobe. The workshop will include hands-on data collection and tutorials with the developers of the Phenix software package.  This event is open to all.
