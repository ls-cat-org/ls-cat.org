The BioNanoprobe will allow 2D and tomographic imaging of subcellular structures and their elemental components with a resolution of less than 50 nm.  This will allow the precise measurement of metallic or metal containing nanoparticle distributions.

The BioNanoprobe is designed to allow operation at cryogenic temperatures to minimize sample damage caused by the intense beam of x-rays.

LS-CAT will make the BioNanoprobe available to investigators at the LS-CAT member institutions.  Time on the instrument will also be available through the APS General User Program.
