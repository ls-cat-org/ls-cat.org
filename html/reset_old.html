Password Reset and Username Selection

 
 

New User Instructions

  1. Enter your APS badge number (numeric part only)
  2. Enter the email address used on a recent ESAF.
  3. Press the "Reset Password" button.
  4. A new password will be assigned to you and sent to the supplied email address.
  5. Retrieve your new password and log in here.
  6. Choose a new user name.
  7. Optional: select a new password.

The username and password you select here is what you'll use to log into the LS-CAT Remote Access Client. If you forget your password or username, just revisit this page and start over by entering your badge number and email address.

Note: The email address entered here must be exactly as entered into a recently approved APS ESAF and be associated with your APS badge number. You cannot access the LS-CAT remote system without being named on an ESAF.
