Here is the original announcement:

Dear LS-CAT Members,


To kick off the availability of robotic sample changers at LS-CAT and other new features, I am pleased to announce a workshop that will take place this April 14 and 15.The workshop will have a lecture component on the 14th covering topics on Beamline Control Software, Data Processing, Low Energy Phasing, and LS-CAT Computer/Software Environment. The second segment on the 15th will be a hands-on session where you can use one of our new robots to mount your samples.

As an incentive for attending, we will be giving away to members two SPINE kits per share. Each kit contains 5 SPINE baskets, a canister for holding the baskets in a dry shipper, vial tongs, and basket tongs.

Here is the workshop agenda: http://docs.google.com/Doc?id=d9fk6c2_504g4fswz74

Note that non-US citizens whose badge has expired or who do not yet have an APS badge must ALSO register here BY MARCH 13: https://beam.aps.anl.gov/pls/apsweb/ufr_main_pkg.usr_start_page

We support samples mounted on SPINE compatible mounts:

http://hamptonresearch.com/product_detail.aspx?cid=24&sid=152&pid=445
or
http://www.moleculardimensions.com/us/merchant.ihtml?new_id=205&step=2

To use the SPINE gripper you MUST use the SPINE vials with the metal disk on the end (Molecular Dimensions MD7-402 or Hampton Research HR8-114). Vials without the metal disk will not work.

To use the Cryotong gripper with Rigaku/ACTOR magazines you may use SPINE compatible caps (HR8-112 or MD7-401, for example) or ALS compatible caps (HR8-182, for example).

In short, for Hampton Research:

  1. CrystalCap HT with vials as noted above work with our SPINE gripper
  2. CrystalCap HT and CrystalCap Copper Magnetic ALS HT work without vials using the cryotong gripper and Rigaku/ACTOR magazines
  3. CrystalCap Copper Magnetic HT series caps DO NOT WORK WITH THE LS-CAT SAMPLE CHANGERS

Currently the screening mode rate for the SPINE gripper is about 28 samples/hour and for the Cryotong gripper about 22 samples/hour.

I hope to see you in April!

Keith
